Subscribe to someone's feed

To follow someone you need their feed's address, and a relay that both of you can reach. This page starts with the relay, since without one you see only your own feed.

A relay you share

The relay your window starts can be reached from your computer only. To read each other, one of you runs a relay that listens on the network, before starting terse-host:

terse-relay --listen 0.0.0.0:7878 --store terse-relay-store

The other adds it once, with the first one's computer name or address on your network, and starts terse-host again:

terse relay add ws://their-computer:7878

A relay has no accounts: anyone who can reach it can read what is on it, but nobody can publish in your name. A relay reached with wss:// (over TLS) also hides from the network what you read; a name alone, as in terse relay add relay.example.org, means wss://relay.example.org. Tersenet has no public relay yet; once there is one, adding it is the same command.

From an address

Ask for their address: what terse feed address prints, or what the reader's help page (?) shows. It is a key of 64 hexadecimal digits, sometimes followed by @ and a relay. In the reader press F for your feeds, then S, and type or paste it (Ctrl+V pastes). Enter subscribes, and the window asks you first:

Tersenet · permission
“reader” asks to subscribe to the feed 73670a…a9ee5f.
Its articles will be downloaded and shown in your timeline.
Allow?   Y yes     N or Esc no

Y, and their articles appear in your timeline: everything they have published, and from now on whatever they publish next. An address with a relay after the @ also finds the feed on that relay if yours do not have it, as long as it is on the internet rather than a home network.

In a terminal, terse feed subscribe and the address does the same (the window then shows the feed the next time it starts).

From a name

If they let search find their name (--listed, in the profile page), search for it: / in the reader, the name, Enter.

Feeds
1 found
Ana
73670a…a9ee5f

Enter on the feed shows its page; S subscribes. In a terminal, terse find Ana lists the feeds of that name with their keys. Names are not unique, so check the key with the person.

Nicknames, unsubscribing, syncing

In the feeds view (F), N gives the selected feed a nickname that only you see, such as "Ana from choir"; an empty one removes it. U unsubscribes. In a terminal: terse feed nickname <key> "Ana from choir" and terse feed unsubscribe <key>.

While the window is open it keeps up by itself. From a terminal, terse feed sync fetches what is new in every feed you follow, and terse feed list lists the entries, one a line: date, feed, entry number, size, whether it is downloaded, and title.
