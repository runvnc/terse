Run Tersenet

Start the window from a terminal:

terse-host

(On Windows, double-click terse-host.exe.) A window opens, and the terminal shows a few lines about it. One says which relay it uses; unless you have set up others, it is one the window starts itself, which only this computer can reach:

terse-host: relay ws://127.0.0.1:7878 (started here, store …)

A relay is where articles are kept and passed on; this one keeps them in the folder relay in ~/.local/share/terse (on Windows %LOCALAPPDATA%\terse). Leave the window open while you follow the next pages: the terse commands in them publish through this relay.

The launcher

The window opens on the launcher, a list of programs:

Up, Down choose · Enter start · the home key comes back here

Up and Down move the cursor, which starts on "reader". Enter starts the program under it in a tile of its own, and that program gets the keyboard.

F12 is the home key. Wherever you are, F12 brings you back to the launcher; no program ever sees it. From there, Enter on another program starts it beside the first, and Esc goes back to the one you were using.

The reader and this manual

Choose "reader" and press Enter. The reader opens on your timeline, the newest articles of the feeds you follow. It is empty for now:

No articles yet
The timeline shows the articles of the feeds this host
subscribes to, newest first. New articles appear here as
they arrive.
Press F to see your feeds and subscribe to one by its key.
Press T to publish a test article of your own; it shows up
here.
Press M to read the Tersenet manual.
Press ? for help, Esc to quit.

Press M: the reader opens the first page of this manual. Tab moves to a link, Enter follows it and Esc goes back. "Manual" in the launcher does the same in one step. The manual is built into Tersenet, so it opens without a network.

Press ? in the reader for every key of every view. Esc on the timeline stops the reader and brings back the launcher; its tile stays, marked ■, until you press X there.

Quit

Close the window. Nothing needs saving: what you publish and follow is kept as you go. (To keep the programs you have open for next time, press S in the launcher first: Find and play a game shows how.)

What you have now

Tersenet running, the launcher, and the reader with this manual in it. Next, make the key that is your identity: Create your profile.

Every key of the launcher, the layouts and the tiles is in Using the shell.
