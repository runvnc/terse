Try Tersenet in your browser

Tersenet is a way to share formatted text, and small programs that run on tiny isolated machines, without web pages or central sites.

The shell with the built-in programs and the manual. About 1 MB to load.
Or one program alone:
Click Start. Then the keyboard goes to the machine: arrows and Enter in the launcher; F12 or the Home button goes back to it.

This is the real machine: terse-core, compiled to WebAssembly, running each program at 60 frames a second within its fixed budget. In the browser it has no network and no sound, and the page is set in your browser's fonts.

Why run it locally

  • Your own feed and key: publish articles and programs under your name.
  • Follow other people's feeds; new entries arrive as they publish, and stay readable offline.
  • The full shell: every program, and programs from the feeds you follow.
  • Real performance, the vector display's full look, and sound.
  • Your named records and saved sessions stay on your computer.

Install on Linux or macOS:

curl -fsSL https://tersenet.org/install.sh | sh
