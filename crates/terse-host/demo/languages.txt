The page device lays out text in any script the host has fonts for, and
each paragraph runs in its own direction. Here is the same short greeting
in three languages.

Hello, and welcome. This article was published from your own feed when the
demo started. If you can read the next two paragraphs, your host found
fonts for Japanese and for Arabic, shaped the Arabic letters so that they
join, and set the Arabic paragraph from right to left.

こんにちは。これはTerseのデモ記事です。この段落は日本語で書かれていて、ホストのフォントで組版されています。日本語には単語の間に空白がないので、行はどの文字の後でも折り返すことができます。

مرحبًا بك. هذه مقالة تجريبية من Terse. تُكتب العربية من اليمين إلى اليسار، وتتصل حروفها ببعضها، ويقوم المضيف بتشكيل النص وعرضه بالاتجاه الصحيح.

A paragraph may also mix them: the word for tea is お茶 (ocha) in Japanese
and شاي (shay) in Arabic, and the line still reads from left to right
because it begins with an English word.
