The page device lays out text in any script its fonts cover, and each
paragraph runs in its own direction. Here is the same short greeting in
three languages.

Hello, and welcome. This article was published from your own feed when the
demo started. The next two paragraphs are in Greek and in Arabic, drawn
with the fonts built into the host: the Arabic letters are shaped so that
they join, and that paragraph is set from right to left.

Γειά σας. Αυτό είναι ένα δοκιμαστικό άρθρο του Tersenet. Η παράγραφος είναι γραμμένη στα ελληνικά και στοιχειοθετείται με τις γραμματοσειρές του προγράμματος.

مرحبًا بك. هذه مقالة تجريبية من Tersenet. تُكتب العربية من اليمين إلى اليسار، وتتصل حروفها ببعضها، ويقوم المضيف بتشكيل النص وعرضه بالاتجاه الصحيح.

A paragraph may also mix them: the word for tea is τσάι (tsai) in Greek
and شاي (shay) in Arabic, and the line still reads from left to right
because it begins with an English word.

Scripts the built-in fonts lack, such as Japanese, Chinese and Korean,
show as boxes until you add a font that has them, with
`terse fonts add <file>`.
